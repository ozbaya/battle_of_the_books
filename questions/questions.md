# Battle of the Books questions

| Question | Book Title | Author |
|---|---|---|
| In which book does a boy dig holes at a camp in the desert? | Holes | Louis Sachar |
| In which book does a spider write words in her web to save a pig? | Charlotte's Web | E.B. White |
| In which book does a boy named Greg keep a diary of his awkward middle school life? | Diary of a Wimpy Kid | Jeff Kinney |
| In which book do four siblings walk through a wardrobe into a magical, snowy land? | The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe | C.S. Lewis |
| In which book does an orphan boy discover he is a wizard and attend a school of magic? | Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone | J.K. Rowling |
| In which book does a girl named Dorothy get swept away by a tornado to a magical land? | The Wonderful Wizard of Oz | L. Frank Baum |
| In which book does a lonely boy build a friendship with a boy and girl across a creek using an imaginary kingdom? | Bridge to Terabithia | Katherine Paterson |
| In which book does a boy named Percy discover he is the son of a Greek god? | Percy Jackson and the Lightning Thief | Rick Riordan |
| In which book does a spoiled girl discover a hidden, neglected garden and help it bloom again? | The Secret Garden | Frances Hodgson Burnett |
| In which book does a family of mice help a princess and foil the schemes of a scheming rat? | The Tale of Despereaux | Kate DiCamillo |
